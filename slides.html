04 — Hearing Health
The Real
Issue.
🔊
Noise-Induced Loss
High-volume listening over extended sessions causes permanent damage that builds silently — especially in users under 35.
🔔
Tinnitus
Ringing or sensitivity is worsened by harsh sound and high volumes. A bad fit forces volume up, compounding damage every session.
👂
Age-Related Loss
Older listeners lose high-frequency hearing first. They need treble clarity and speech-first tuning at safe, lower volumes.
🌊
Background Masking
Poorly-sealed designs force users to raise volume in noise — directly increasing cumulative hearing risk without them realizing it.
😓
Listening Fatigue
Heavy bass and poor ergonomics cause ear fatigue quickly. Harsh resonance makes users remove headphones early, reducing productive listening time.
🗣
Speech Clarity Gaps
For hearing-impaired users, voices matter more than bass. Most headphones tune for 'fun' sound, leaving this segment completely underserved.

06 — Opportunities
Where Beats
Leads Next.
01
Hearing-Aware Presets
App-based EQ presets — "Speech", "Clear Voice", "Low Fatigue" — targeting users with hearing differences.
02
Safe-Listening Alerts
Volume time reminders and smart alerts to reduce noise-induced hearing risk for heavy daily users.
03
Comfort-First Engineering
Redesign cushioning and clamp pressure — the #1 recurring complaint across all product lines.
04
Hearing-Aware Mode
Intelligently lower volume in quiet environments so users stop compensating in noisy ones.
05
Treble Clarity Options
Dedicated tuning for age-related hearing loss — better speech definition at safe, lower volumes.
06
App Redesign
Clean, frictionless UI — Sony is actively losing users over bad software. This is a clear open lane.
07
Ear Health Positioning
Own the narrative: position Beats as the brand that protects your hearing, not just moves it — a meaningful, ownable differentiator in a crowded market.
07 — Reflection
The Process
Behind It.
Student: Basheer Khan Date: June 2026 Tools: Perplexity AI, Claude LLM
Question 01
What steps worked well?
Approaching research from two angles — company-internal and outside consumer — helped ask better questions. Converting raw review volume into structured frequency percentages turned anecdotal feedback into visual evidence.
Question 02
What roadblocks did you encounter?
The key decision: stop at a basic chart, or push further for something more complete? Perplexity AI provided general summaries but lacked the depth and focus needed to drive the research forward effectively.
Question 03
How did you solve those issues?
Transitioned to Claude — a more capable LLM — which could synthesize findings, identify segment patterns, build frequency data, and surface the hearing health angle that became the real opportunity for Beats.
The Takeaway
Premium sound.

Premium hearing care.

The market is saturated with headphones that move sound. The brand that owns hearing health owns the next decade. That brand should be Beats.
